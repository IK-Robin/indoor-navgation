🏥 Indoor Navigation: Live Turn Test

QR checkpoint substitute + motion steps + heading-based junction turns. Runs on GitHub Pages over HTTPS.

Experimental: the phone may detect steps while its orientation is inaccurate. This app estimates turns only at mapped junctions; it does not know your exact indoor location. Keep your phone facing your walking direction for this test.

Calibration: hold the phone upright with its top pointing along the corridor you are about to walk. Tap “Calibrate forward heading” while stationary. Keep phone orientation consistent.

Map scale: 10 SVG pixels ≈ 1 meter. At a junction, turn your whole body and keep the phone pointing ahead. If the turn cannot be determined, use a manual direction button.
