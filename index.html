Indoor QR + Phone Motion Test

A small, GitHub Pages-ready proof of concept. Scan a known-location QR code, allow motion sensors, and watch an estimated blue dot move on the corridor map.

Ready. For actual sensors, open on a phone using HTTPS.

Camera QR scanning uses BarcodeDetector when supported. Otherwise use a phone's normal camera app to open the QR URL, or enter the checkpoint manually.

For this test, select the direction manually. Compass readings are shown but will not reverse movement automatically.

Keep the phone steady while walking. Step detection is experimental. Use the direction dropdown to choose which way the blue dot moves; compass heading is diagnostic only.

Map scale: 20 SVG pixels per meter. The blue dot moves one estimated stride per detected step in the direction selected above. This proof of concept constrains movement to one straight corridor. It does not implement full 2D pathfinding, reliable floor detection, or clinical-grade positioning.
